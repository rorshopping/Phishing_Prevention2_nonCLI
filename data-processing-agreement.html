Data Processing Agreement

Auftragsverarbeitungsvertrag gemäß Art. 28 DSGVO

This DPA is included with every PhishDefend AI contract. The signed version is provided during onboarding. This page is for informational reference.

1. Parties

Controller (Verantwortlicher): [Client Company Name, Address]

Processor (Auftragsverarbeiter): PhishDefend AI — [Provider Address]

2. Subject Matter and Duration

The Processor processes personal data on behalf of the Controller for the purpose of conducting simulated phishing campaigns and security awareness training. The agreement remains in effect for the duration of the service contract.

3. Nature and Purpose of Processing

The Processor processes employee names, email addresses, job roles, and phishing simulation results (clicks, submissions, reports) solely for:

  • Creating and executing personalized phishing simulations
  • Generating aggregated risk reports
  • Assigning and tracking security training
  • Providing anonymized benchmarking data

4. Categories of Data Subjects

Employees, contractors, and other personnel of the Controller who are enrolled in the phishing prevention program.

5. Technical and Organizational Measures (TOMs)

  • All data encrypted in transit (TLS 1.3) and at rest (AES-256)
  • Data hosted on EU servers (Hetzner, Germany)
  • Pseudonymization of personal data via salted hashing
  • Access controls with MFA for all administrative accounts
  • Automated data retention and deletion (90 days for raw results, 7 days for logs)
  • Regular security audits and penetration testing

6. Sub-processors

  • Hetzner GmbH (hosting, Germany)
  • OpenAI / OpenRouter (LLM processing, EU/US — data anonymized before transmission)

7. Data Subject Rights

The Processor shall assist the Controller in fulfilling its obligations to respond to data subject requests under Articles 15–22 GDPR. Requests must be forwarded to the Controller within 48 hours.

8. Data Breach Notification

The Processor will notify the Controller of any personal data breach within 24 hours of discovery, in accordance with Art. 33 GDPR.

9. Deletion and Return

Upon termination of the agreement, the Processor will delete all personal data within 30 days unless EU law requires retention. The Controller may request a final export of data before deletion.
